# United States — free, machine-readable sources for holidays, school calendars and waste collection (as of Sept 2026)

## Public holidays: federal, observed-date rules, state holidays; compute or fetch?

### Takeaway
The 11 federal holidays are fixed in 5 U.S.C. 6103 as simple date/weekday rules, so they can be **computed in-app** exactly like `german_holidays.dart`. State holidays vary a lot (Good Friday, Lincoln's Birthday, Columbus vs. Indigenous Peoples' Day, Truman Day…) and are best **ported from the MIT-licensed `python-holidays` rule set** (it covers all 50 states + DC + territories) rather than fetched. Neither federal nor state holidays tell a family whether *school* is closed; that comes only from the district calendar.

### Cited Findings
- 5 U.S.C. 6103(a) lists exactly 11 legal public holidays: New Year's Day (Jan 1); Birthday of Martin Luther King Jr. (3rd Monday Jan); Washington's Birthday (3rd Monday Feb); Memorial Day (last Monday May); Juneteenth National Independence Day (June 19); Independence Day (July 4); Labor Day (1st Monday Sep); Columbus Day (2nd Monday Oct); Veterans Day (Nov 11); Thanksgiving Day (4th Thursday Nov); Christmas Day (Dec 25). — [LII / 5 U.S.C. 6103](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/5/6103)
- 6103(b): for Mon–Fri employees, "Instead of a holiday that occurs on a Saturday, the Friday immediately before is a legal public holiday"; the Sunday→Monday rule comes from Executive Order 11582 / the same section as applied by OPM. — [LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/5/6103); [OPM federal holidays](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/pay-leave/federal-holidays/)
- OPM: "when a holiday falls on a nonworkday -- Saturday or Sunday -- the holiday usually is observed on Monday (if the holiday falls on Sunday) or Friday (if the holiday falls on Saturday)". OPM lists 2024–2030 (history back to 2011). Inauguration Day (Jan 20 every 4th year) is a holiday only for federal employees in the D.C. area. — [OPM](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/pay-leave/federal-holidays/)
- OPM offers one machine-readable format: an iCalendar download at `/policy-data-oversight/pay-leave/federal-holidays/holidays.ics`; no JSON/CSV/API. — [OPM](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/pay-leave/federal-holidays/). **Verified by test (2026-09-18): a plain `curl` of that .ics returned an Akamai "Access Denied" page**, so it is not reliably fetchable server-side without a browser-like client.
- Juneteenth was added to the schedule in 2021. — [OPM](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/pay-leave/federal-holidays/)
- Nager.Date public API (`https://date.nager.at/api/v3/PublicHolidays/2026/US`, no key) returns federal holidays with `global: true` and state holidays with a `counties` list of ISO codes (e.g. Good Friday 2026-04-03 for US-CT, DE, HI, IN, KY, LA, NC, ND, NJ, TN; Lincoln's Birthday for 9 states; Truman Day US-MO; Columbus Day as Public in 33 states and as Bank nationwide; Indigenous Peoples' Day in 19 states). It returns **observed** dates (Independence Day 2026 shown as 2026-07-03, since July 4, 2026 is a Saturday). — tested live against [date.nager.at API](https://date.nager.at/api/v3/PublicHolidays/2026/US); the site now redirects to nagerholidays.com.
- `python-holidays` (vacanza/holidays) `UnitedStates` class defines 57 subdivisions (50 states, DC, AS, GU, MP, PR, UM, VI) and categories GOVERNMENT, HALF_DAY, PUBLIC, UNOFFICIAL; the license is the MIT-style permissive text ("Permission is hereby granted, free of charge… to use, copy, modify, merge, publish, distribute, sublicense, and/or sell"). — [united_states.py](https://github.com/vacanza/holidays/blob/dev/holidays/countries/united_states.py); [LICENSE](https://github.com/vacanza/holidays/blob/dev/LICENSE)
- Nager.Date also tags federal holidays with type `Bank` (Columbus Day and Veterans Day are Federal Reserve bank holidays nationwide even where not state holidays). — [Nager.Date API](https://date.nager.at/api/v3/PublicHolidays/2026/US)

### Inferences
- Federal: compute in Dart (11 rules + Sat→Fri / Sun→Mon shift, mirroring the German approach). No network dependency needed; OPM's .ics is blocked to bots anyway.
- State: port the per-state rules from python-holidays (MIT, attribution in licenses screen) into a Dart table keyed by state code; this matches the "fixed in law, compute it" principle. Nager.Date is a fine cross-check / test oracle but a third-party runtime dependency is not needed. Note state holidays change by statute (e.g. Columbus → Indigenous Peoples' Day renamings), so the table needs yearly review.
- What US families care about: school closures (district calendar, not law), plus bank/post-office closures (= federal list). State holidays mostly affect state offices; many states' "holidays" (Lincoln's Birthday, Truman Day) are observances few families notice. Suggest showing federal holidays by default and state holidays as an optional layer.
- Unlike Germany, there is no US analogue to "Feiertag = everything closed"; private employers and schools choose which to observe.

### Gaps
- Did not find an official, government-published machine-readable dataset of *state* holidays (each state legislates its own; no federal compilation found).
- Could not verify OPM's .ics terms; as a US federal work it is public domain (17 U.S.C. 105) — inference, not checked on the page.

## School calendars: district feeds, aggregators, address→district resolution

### Takeaway
There are **13,000+ geographically defined public school districts**, each setting its own calendar; there is **no free, official, nationwide machine-readable school-calendar dataset**. Most district website CMS platforms (Finalsite, SchoolMessenger Presence, Edlio, Apptegy/Thrillshare, Google Calendar embeds) do expose **per-calendar iCal subscription URLs**, so the realistic free route is the one Aporah already has: **the user pastes their district's ICS link**. Address→district resolution is free and public domain (Census Geocoder with school-district layers, NCES EDGE boundaries), which can be used to *suggest* the district and a link to its calendar page.

### Cited Findings
- "The United States has more than 13,000 geographically defined public school districts." NCES EDGE publishes annually updated boundary composites (latest 2024–2025 school year) as shapefiles and ArcGIS web services; types: unified, elementary, secondary, Vermont supervisory, plus pseudo-districts; NYC, Hawaii and Puerto Rico are single districts. — [NCES EDGE District Boundaries](https://nces.ed.gov/programs/edge/Geographic/DistrictBoundaries)
- NCES composite file documentation: "All information contained in this file is in the public domain." Boundaries come from the Census School District Review Program (state officials) via TIGER. — [EDGE composite file doc (PDF)](https://nces.ed.gov/programs/edge/docs/EDGE_SDBOUNDARIES_COMPOSITE_FILEDOC.pdf); REST MapServer e.g. [EDGE_SCHOOLDISTRICT_TL23_SY2223](https://nces.ed.gov/opengis/rest/services/School_District_Boundaries/EDGE_SCHOOLDISTRICT_TL23_SY2223/MapServer/0); [data.gov listing](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/school-district-boundaries-current-869e3)
- Census Geocoder endpoints: `onelineaddress`, `address` (street/city/state/zip), `addressPR`, `coordinates`; geographies include State, County, Tract, Block, places, etc., and **Unified / Elementary / Secondary School Districts as optional `layers`**; batch limit 10,000 records; no API key documented. — [Census Geocoding Services API](https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/Geocoding_Services_API.html)
- Live test (2026-09-18): `geographies/onelineaddress?address=1600 Pennsylvania Ave NW, Washington, DC 20500&benchmark=Public_AR_Current&vintage=Current_Current&layers=Unified School Districts,Counties&format=json` returned County GEOID 11001 and Unified School District "District of Columbia Public Schools", GEOID 1100030 (= NCES LEA ID), LOGRADE PK / HIGRADE 12, with matched coordinates and ZIP. — [geocoding.geo.census.gov](https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder)
- Census API terms: attribution text required ("This product uses the Census Bureau Data API but is not endorsed or certified by the Census Bureau"); no endorsement implied; the Bureau may impose/enforce access limits; no prohibition on commercial use; provided "as is". — [Census API Terms of Service](https://www.census.gov/data/developers/terms-of-service.html) (note: these are the Data API terms; the geocoder page itself states no separate ToS).
- Finalsite (large K-12 CMS): calendar pages have a subscribe icon opening "Calendar Feeds" with "Get Standard iCal URL", "Get Google iCal URL", or "Add to Default Calendar App"; users can subscribe to all or individual calendars. — [Finalsite Support: Subscribe to a calendar](https://www.finalsitesupport.com/hc/en-us/articles/27762554995981-Subscribe-to-a-calendar); [Help your community subscribe](https://www.finalsitesupport.com/hc/en-us/articles/30172510365453-Help-your-community-subscribe-to-calendars)
- Districts on SchoolMessenger Presence and similar CMSs publish "Subscribe to Calendar" pages with iCal URLs (examples: Torrance USD, Shrewsbury MA, Northport NY). — [TUSD subscribe](https://www.tusd.org/calendar/subscribe); [Shrewsbury](https://schools.shrewsburyma.gov/district/calendar/subscribe); [Northport](https://northportps.org/about-us/district-calendar/subscribe/using-ical---google-calendar/)
- Edlio CMS "integrates with Google & iCal"; school calendars can import from Google Calendar. — [Edlio Help: Calendars](https://help.edlio.com/apps/pages/calendars); [Edlio CMS](https://edlio.com/apps/pages/edliocms)
- Apptegy/Thrillshare: calendars connect with Google and Microsoft Calendar to feed website and app. — [Apptegy platform](https://www.apptegy.com/thrillshare-platform/)
- Commercial aggregators exist: schools-calendar.com advertises a "School Calendar API" returning first/last day, breaks and holidays as JSON, sourced from district websites, PDFs and iCal feeds (e.g. 834 Texas districts); myschooldates.com aggregates "America's largest public school districts" with calendar import. — [schools-calendar.com](https://schools-calendar.com/); [TX page](https://schools-calendar.com/school-calendar/tx/); [MySchoolDates](https://myschooldates.com/). Pricing/terms could not be read (site returned 403 to fetch) — **treat as paid/unknown, not free**.

### Inferences
- Pasted-ICS is the only free, legal, scalable path; it reuses the existing `ical` tile/`calendar-link` mechanism unchanged. A US onboarding hint could say "Your district website → Calendar → Subscribe → copy iCal link".
- Useful helper: geocode the household address once (Census, server-side in an Edge Function), store the NCES LEA ID/district name, and show the district name + "find the Subscribe link on its website". NCES CCD directory has district website URLs (not verified in this session) which could deep-link.
- District feeds are often "everything" feeds (athletics, board meetings); families may want the "District Calendar"/"Academic Calendar" feed specifically — worth instructions.
- Unlike German Ferien, a "shared feed per region" model does not fit: the region is the district, and there is no authoritative source to fetch per district. Could still dedupe identical pasted URLs server-side, but that is optimisation.
- Private/charter schools are outside NCES boundary geography entirely; paste-a-link covers them too.

### Gaps
- No free, official nationwide dataset of school calendar dates found. Share of districts that expose ICS vs. PDF-only is unknown (no survey found); PDF-only calendars are common anecdotally but unverified.
- Blackboard/Anthology (now Finalsite-owned? not verified) and Apptegy ICS specifics not confirmed from vendor docs (Apptegy KB page 404).
- Census Geocoder per-request rate limits are undocumented.

## Waste collection: platforms, city open data, per-address machine-readable schedules and terms

### Takeaway
US waste collection is fragmented across city departments and private haulers (WM, Republic, Waste Connections…). The biggest multi-city platform is **ReCollect (owned by Routeware since 2021)**, which serves a per-address **ICS feed** (`…/api/places/<place>/services/<id>/events.en.ics`) — used by Houston, Austin, Denver, Recology SF, Cambridge, Raleigh, Waste Connections, etc. Others: **Recycle Coach** (JSON API), **Recycle By City** (ICS, Chicago/Flagstaff), plus bespoke city portals (NYC DSNY, Seattle, LA, San Diego, Philadelphia…). **None of these has a published public API or data license for third-party commercial reuse**; the community (Home Assistant `waste_collection_schedule`) relies on undocumented endpoints "at your own risk". City open data (e.g. NYC) usually publishes **zone polygons/frequencies, not per-address dated schedules**.

### Cited Findings
- Home Assistant `hacs_waste_collection_schedule` lists ~75 US sources, including generic ones: ReCollect (ICS), Recycle Coach, Recycle By City (ICS), Republic Services, Waste Management (WM), Waste Connections (via ReCollect), and cities incl. NYC, Seattle, LA (lacitysan.org), San Diego, San Antonio, Philadelphia, Pittsburgh, Minneapolis, Kansas City, Oklahoma City, Baltimore County, Prince George's County, Jacksonville, Orange County FL; ReCollect-backed: Austin, Denver, Houston, Orlando, Raleigh, Cambridge, Lowell, McKinney, Davenport, Roseville, Sacramento County, Recology San Francisco, Rogue Disposal, Hardin Sanitation, University Park TX, Apopka, Gastonia, Georgetown TX. — [HACS README](https://github.com/mampfes/hacs_waste_collection_schedule/blob/master/README.md)
- ReCollect per-address ICS: search address → "Get a calendar" → "Add to Google Calendar" yields e.g. `https://recollect.a.ssl.fastly.net/api/places/<UUID>/services/208/events.en.ics?client_id=…`; the client_id can be stripped. — [HACS ReCollect doc](https://github.com/mampfes/hacs_waste_collection_schedule/blob/master/doc/ics/recollect.md)
- Home Assistant's ReCollect integration "uses the ReCollect API URL to obtain data and not an official API from ReCollect. Use at your own risk." — [Home Assistant ReCollect Waste](https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/recollect_waste/); library [aiorecollect](https://github.com/bachya/aiorecollect)
- Routeware (Banneker Partners portfolio) acquired Vancouver-based ReCollect on 2021-03-25. `recollect.net/terms/` now redirects to routeware.com. — [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2021/03/25/2199529/0/en/Routeware-Global-Acquires-ReCollect-to-Add-Digital-Engagement-Expertise.html)
- ReCollect Terms of Service (operated by "Routeware, Inc., a Delaware corporation", jurisdiction British Columbia courts): the fetched text contains **no clauses on automated access, scraping, API use, redistribution or commercial reuse** (neither permission nor prohibition). — [ReCollect ToS](https://api.recollect.net/terms.en-US.html)
- ReCollect publishes an import-side "data formats" help page for municipalities (how cities give *it* data), not an outbound API. — [ReCollect help: data waste formats](https://recollect.net/help-data-waste-formats/)
- Recycle Coach: address lookup (street, city, state) against an undocumented JSON API `https://api-city.recyclecoach.com/city/search?term=<city>, <state>, USA` returning `project_id`, `district_id`, then zone schedules. — [HACS Recycle Coach doc](https://github.com/mampfes/hacs_waste_collection_schedule/blob/master/doc/source/recyclecoach_com.md)
- Recycle By City: ICS subscribe URLs per address (`webcal://www.recyclebycity.com/chicago/schedule/<hash>/subscribe`), working only for Chicago and Flagstaff. — [HACS Recycle By City doc](https://github.com/mampfes/hacs_waste_collection_schedule/blob/master/doc/ics/recyclebycity_com.md)
- Waste Management (WM): uses WM's public guest address lookup (street, city, 2-letter state, 5-digit ZIP; no MyWM account); returns trash/recycling/yard with holiday shifts; "WM's internal API keys are embedded in their web application and may be rotated periodically." — [HACS WM doc](https://github.com/mampfes/hacs_waste_collection_schedule/blob/master/doc/source/wm_com.md)
- Republic Services: single `street_address` string input, returns dated Recycle / Solid Waste / Yard / Bulk pickups. — [HACS Republic doc](https://github.com/mampfes/hacs_waste_collection_schedule/blob/master/doc/source/republicservices_com.md)
- NYC: DSNY's address lookup form (`nyc.gov/assets/dsny/forms/collection-schedule`) takes a mailing address; also in the DSNY Pickup and NYC 311 apps. — [HACS NYC doc](https://github.com/mampfes/hacs_waste_collection_schedule/blob/master/doc/source/nyc_gov.md); [DSNY Collection Schedule](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/dsny/forms/collection-schedule)
- NYC Open Data "Garbage Collection Schedule" (p7k6-2pm8) = "Citywide DSNY frequency boundaries for collection operation (refuse, recycling, organics, bulk items). These boundaries are sub-divisions of DSNY sections." Metadata: agency DSNY, update frequency "Monthly", automation "No"; rows last updated April 2024 (timestamp 1712743896); no license field set. It is a polygon/frequency layer (weekday pattern per zone), not per-address dated events. — [NYC Open Data](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/City-Government/Garbage-Collection-Schedule/p7k6-2pm8); [data.gov DSNY Frequencies](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/dsny-frequencies) (queried via Socrata metadata API)
- Seattle Public Utilities: street-address lookup against `myutilities.seattle.gov` eportal (road type and direction required; `prem_code` fallback). — [HACS Seattle doc](https://github.com/mampfes/hacs_waste_collection_schedule/blob/master/doc/source/seattle_gov.md)

### Inferences
- **Architecture fit:** the German `abfall/vendors/` registry maps well: one adapter each for ReCollect, Recycle Coach, WM, Republic, plus top cities (NYC, Seattle, LA, San Diego, Philadelphia). ReCollect alone would cover a large share of big US cities (and Canada) with one ICS adapter — the ICS URL is per *place*, so the "shared feed per address" model applies directly.
- **Legal risk is higher than in Germany:** none of these vendors grants a reuse license; ReCollect's ToS is silent (not a grant), and WM/Republic endpoints are internal web-app APIs with embedded keys. Commercial use in a freemium app is an unresolved legal question — contacting Routeware/ReCollect for a partnership, or limiting to user-pasted ICS links (the user fetches their own schedule), would be safer.
- **Low-risk free path:** the existing "paste an ICS link / upload .ics" flow works for ReCollect and Recycle By City users today with zero adapters — the user copies "Add to Google Calendar" from their city's ReCollect widget.
- City open data (NYC, and likely others) is generally public domain/open but gives weekday-per-zone rules; computing dated schedules from them needs holiday-shift rules per city (DSNY, e.g., has its own holiday schedule). Doable for NYC as a rule-based adapter.
- Many US households on private subscription haulers or rural drop-off have no schedule source at all; the "Anfragen" queue concept transfers.

### Gaps
- No published US-wide census of which platform each city uses; ReCollect's customer count not verified.
- Did not verify WM/Republic website Terms of Use (likely prohibit automated access — unverified).
- Did not verify Seattle/SF/Chicago/LA open-data per-address datasets; only NYC checked (zones, not dates). Recology SF uses ReCollect (per HACS), not open data.
- Rubbish/other small platforms (e.g. "Rubbish", Municipal "My Waste" apps) not researched.

## Free US geocoders and the onboarding address step

### Takeaway
The **Census Geocoder** is free, keyless, public-sector and returns state, county, place and **school district (NCES LEA ID)** from one street address in one call — ideal server-side (Edge Function) for resolving the household. Onboarding should capture a **full street address (street + city + 2-letter state + 5-digit ZIP)**: state alone suffices for holidays, but school district and waste need the street address; ZIP codes cross district and hauler boundaries.

### Cited Findings
- Census Geocoder: structured `address` endpoint takes street, city, state, zip; `onelineaddress` takes a single string; `coordinates` reverse-geocodes lat/lon to geographies; school district layers selectable. — [Census Geocoding Services API](https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/Geocoding_Services_API.html)
- Live test returned state, county GEOID, unified school district + grade range, lat/lon, and parsed ZIP for a single address. — [geocoding.geo.census.gov](https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder)
- Terms: attribution line required, no endorsement, rate limits may be imposed, "as is". — [Census API ToS](https://www.census.gov/data/developers/terms-of-service.html)
- WM's lookup needs street, city, 2-letter state and 5-digit ZIP; Republic and NYC a single full address; Recycle Coach street + city + state; Seattle needs road type and direction exactly. — HACS docs: [WM](https://github.com/mampfes/hacs_waste_collection_schedule/blob/master/doc/source/wm_com.md), [Republic](https://github.com/mampfes/hacs_waste_collection_schedule/blob/master/doc/source/republicservices_com.md), [Recycle Coach](https://github.com/mampfes/hacs_waste_collection_schedule/blob/master/doc/source/recyclecoach_com.md), [Seattle](https://github.com/mampfes/hacs_waste_collection_schedule/blob/master/doc/source/seattle_gov.md)
- Some areas have elementary + secondary districts instead of one unified district, so an address can map to two districts. — [NCES EDGE](https://nces.ed.gov/programs/edge/Geographic/DistrictBoundaries)

### Inferences
- Onboarding fields: street line, city, state (picker of 50 + DC, also drives state holidays), ZIP (5-digit). Store the Census-returned county FIPS and NCES district GEOIDs (may be two) server-side.
- Privacy: geocoding a home address is personal data; doing it in an Edge Function against a US federal service is a third-country (US) transfer for EU-hosted Supabase — acceptable for US users but worth noting in the privacy policy. Census attribution line must appear in the app's licenses/about.
- Census Geocoder is TIGER address-range based: new-build addresses may fail to match; fall back to "choose your state" and manual district search (NCES REST query by name).

### Gaps
- Census Geocoder throughput limits and uptime SLA are undocumented; third parties (Geocodio) note outages — [Geocodio](https://www.geocod.io/census-bureau-api-down-alternative) (vendor marketing, low weight).
- Other free geocoders (Nominatim/OSM with its 1 req/s policy, Photon) not evaluated here.
